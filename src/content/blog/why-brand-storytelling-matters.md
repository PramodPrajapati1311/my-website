Most marketing tells people what a company sells. The work that lasts tells
them why it exists.

## Start with the why

Before a single photograph is taken or a single caption is written, there is a
question worth sitting with: what does this brand actually stand for? Not the
mission statement on the wall. The thing the founder says when the recorder is
off.

## Show the real thing

Staged photography is easy to spot and easy to forget. The frames people
remember are the ones where something actually happened - a conversation
mid-sentence, a room that filled up, a person who was genuinely glad to be
there.

## Make it matter

Every campaign, design, and event should be able to answer one question: what
changes because this exists? If there is no answer, the work is decoration.
